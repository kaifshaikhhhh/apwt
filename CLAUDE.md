## Keep both languages in sync

This site is bilingual: English lives at `/` (`_pages/`), German at `/de/` (`_pages/de/`).
Whenever you are asked to add, update, or remove content — news items, publications, bio, any page — apply the same change to **both** language versions in the same pass. Never update one and leave the other stale.
Write the German by hand in natural, formal German (use *Sie*); keep paper titles, venue names, and established ML terms (Large Language Models, Trustworthy Machine Learning, etc.) in English. Do not machine-translate.
